# Subprocessors

*Reviewed: 12 August 2026. This is a self-assessment; no supervisory authority certifies analytics tools.*\
The subprocessors of Sealmetrics are listed in **Annex 3 of the Data Processing Agreement** at [sealmetrics.com/dpa](https://sealmetrics.com/dpa/), which is the authoritative, always-current version of the list. As of this review it names Noraina Limited (Ireland — infrastructure and database hosting), Scaleway SAS (Paris — managed LLM inference for Seal AI) and Resend (US, under SCCs — service emails); the DPA itself is the reference if the two ever differ.

## How do I get notified of subprocessor changes?

To subscribe to subprocessor change notifications, email **privacy@sealmetrics.com** with the
subject "Subprocessor updates".
