# Sealmetrics Docs — Consentless Web Analytics

Canonical page: https://docs.sealmetrics.com

**Sealmetrics** is a consentless web analytics platform that measures your traffic without cookies or consent banners — designed for GDPR and ePrivacy and self-assessed against the CNIL and AEPD criteria for consent-exempt audience measurement. That is our self-assessment, not a certification, and in Germany the consent question is open — see [Germany](/compliance/germany-ttdsg-self-assessment).

These docs cover everything you need to implement, operate, and build on top of Sealmetrics: tracker installation, the HTTP and JavaScript APIs, every built-in report, compliance posture, and CMS/framework integrations.

## Why Sealmetrics

- **Privacy by architecture** — no cookies, no consent banner for its own analytics (self-assessed; Germany: open question), and we measure all the traffic you lose today to the cookie banner.
- **Legitimate interest by design** — checked criterion by criterion in our public [regulator self-assessments](/compliance).
- **Unified metrics** — dashboard, ROAS, funnels, attribution, and raw exports share one definition layer.
- **24-month retention** without consent, within GDPR bounds.
